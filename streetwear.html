What Streetwear Means to Me

Streetwear is casual clothing that mixes comfort with creativity. It grew out of skate, hip-hop, and basketball culture, and today it is worn all over the world. What I love most is the culture behind it and the creativity it allows. For me, it is about expressing who you are through what you wear.

Pieces I Love

Oversized Hoodies
Roomy, comfortable, and easy to layer, with a relaxed look.
Cropped Hoodies
A shorter cut that changes the shape of an outfit and adds a fresh twist.
Boxy T-Shirts
A wide, square fit that looks clean on its own or under a jacket.
Sneakers
The centerpiece of most outfits. My favorite pair right now is the Off-White Dunks in electric green.

My Tips for Building an Outfit

  • Start with a pair of sneakers you love and build around them.
  • Choose one statement piece, like bright green sneakers, and keep the rest simple.
  • Mix loose pieces, like an oversized hoodie, with a fit that feels balanced.
  • Always choose comfort and fit over trends.
  • Be original, because your style should feel like you.

Basketball and streetwear are closely connected. Many of the sneakers and jerseys people wear every day began on the court.
