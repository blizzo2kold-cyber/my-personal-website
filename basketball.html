My Life with Basketball

Basketball is more than a sport to me. It teaches discipline, teamwork, and confidence. Every game is different, and there is always something to learn, whether you win or lose.

My Basketball Journey

  1. I started playing basketball when I was little.
  2. From fourth grade to seventh grade, I played recreational basketball.
  3. In high school, I switched to track and field instead of organized basketball.
  4. Today, I play for fun and to relieve stress.

My Favorite Player: Kevin Durant

Kevin Durant is my favorite basketball player. He is a tall forward who is known for his smooth shooting and his ability to score from all over the court. I always liked his playing style, and when I was little, I tried to copy his moves in my real games. Watching him made me want to be creative and confident every time I touched the ball.
I do not have one favorite team, because I enjoy watching great basketball from every team.

Why I Love Basketball

  • It is a great way to relieve stress.
  • The fast pace keeps every game exciting.
  • It brings people together, on the court and in the stands.
  • You can practice alone or with friends at any time.
  • Every player can develop their own style of play.

The Five Positions

Point Guard
The floor leader who handles the ball and sets up the offense.
Shooting Guard
A scorer who is skilled at shooting from long range.
Small Forward
A versatile player who can score, defend, and rebound.
Power Forward
A strong player who works near the basket and grabs rebounds.
Center
The tallest player, who protects the rim and scores inside.
