Welcome to My Website

Hi, and welcome! My name is Samuel Ankrah, and this website is all about the two things I enjoy most outside of the classroom: basketball and streetwear fashion. I built this site as my first web design project, and I wanted it to show who I am and what I care about.

Basketball

I have been playing basketball since I was little. From fourth grade through seventh grade, I played recreational basketball, and those years taught me the basics of teamwork, effort, and having fun with the game. When I got to high school, I stopped playing organized basketball and joined the track and field team instead. Today, I still play just for fun, and it is one of the best ways for me to relieve stress after a long week of classes.

My favorite basketball player is Kevin Durant. I have always loved his playing style, and when I was younger I tried to copy many of his moves in my actual games. I do not have a favorite team, because I enjoy watching great basketball no matter who is playing.

Streetwear

Streetwear is my other passion. What I like most is the culture behind it and the creativity it allows. To me, fashion is a way to express your personality without saying a word. I love oversized hoodies, cropped hoodies, and boxy t-shirts, and my favorite sneakers right now are the Off-White Dunks in electric green. Basketball and streetwear have always gone hand in hand, since so many players and fans have shaped what people wear every day.

On the Basketball page, you can read about my basketball journey and the positions on the court. On the Streetwear page, you can learn about the pieces I wear and some tips for putting together an outfit. If you would like to get in touch, please visit the Contact page.
